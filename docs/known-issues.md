# Known issues

The ledger for every error, warning, defect, gap, and **improvement opportunity** encountered in Curfew's orbit — this package, the harnesses it runs alongside, the toolchain, and this machine. **Nothing is ignored for being "not my code," pre-existing, or someone else's repo.** An item is worked or explicitly deferred by the owner, and it closes only with evidence.

Severity: `blocker` (stops the build or daily use) · `defect` (wrong behaviour, needs a fix) · `gap` (missing something expected) · `improvement` (works, but should or could be better — logged on encounter, triaged by the owner, never silently scope-creeped into the running slice) · `environmental` (outside the code, owner action) · `risk` (no defect yet, exposure exists). These six are the whole vocabulary — a taxonomy with meanings, not a free tag. `scripts/ledger_check.py` polices the columns, the id sequence, and the status cell.
